From a folder
to its sources.

  1. Choose what Loft can read

    Download a chat model and an embedding model, then add a folder. Loft indexes its contents and keeps up with changes.

  2. Ask from your desktop

    Press Ctrl+Shift+Space on Windows or Cmd+Shift+Space on Mac to open Loft and ask about your documents or code.

  3. Check the original

    Expand the sources beneath a reply to read the excerpts. Open a document or reveal a source file in its folder.

Build your own Loft.

Start with the sample documents or run the notch UI with mock data. The MIT-licensed source includes the Tauri desktop app and its local llama.cpp integration.
